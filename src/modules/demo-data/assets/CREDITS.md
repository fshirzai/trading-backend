# Demo product photography

These are illustrative product photographs, not photographs of company stock. All three are by Fumikas Sagisavas, released under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They were resized to 900 pixels and converted to WebP, with metadata removed.

- beans.webp: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Red_kidney_beans.jpg
- chickpeas.webp: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chickpeas_(1).jpg
- lentils.webp: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Red_lentils_(1).jpg

Source image bytes are bundled; seeding does not depend on an external photo service or accept arbitrary image URLs.
